# Sound library

Real recorded sounds for the mixer (`explainer/mix.py`), so videos do not rely only on
synthesized effects. Every file here must have a licence that allows use in published videos.

## kenney/ (CC0, no credit needed)

Kenney (www.kenney.nl) packs: interface-sounds, ui-audio, digital-audio, impact-sounds.
Each folder keeps its `License.txt`. Used for accents: zoom in and out (`maximize`, `minimize`),
headlines landing (`impactSoft`), connections (`pluck`), checks (`tick`, `confirmation`).

## incoming/ (owner downloads)

Drop downloads here with the page URL in a `sources.txt` line per file. Wanted:

1. **Stylus writing on a tablet (Apple Pencil on iPad glass)**: 2 or 3 clips, 5 to 20 s each,
   writing and short strokes. Used under every handwritten note.
2. **Marker or felt pen on paper, scribbles**: 2 clips, short. Used when a line or circle draws.
3. **Soft whooshes**: 4 to 6 clips, 0.4 to 1 s, airy, not sci-fi. Used when the camera zooms.
4. **Background music**: 2 calm instrumental tracks, 2 to 3 min, no vocals (ambient, lofi or
   soft piano). Used under the voice, lowered while she speaks.
5. **One soft reveal**: a gentle riser or a warm swell, 2 to 4 s, for the final zoom out and the logo.

Good sources: Pixabay sound effects and music (Pixabay licence, no credit needed) or Freesound
filtered to CC0. If a file is CC BY, add its credit line to the post caption.
