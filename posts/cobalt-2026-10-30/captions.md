## post-p5: will it fit? (ref 77)
Will a 7B model fit on your laptop?

At 16-bit, the weights are 7 billion x 2 bytes = 14 GB. Squeezed to 4-bit, about 3.5 GB.
That is the weights only; the chat itself (the KV cache) needs more on top.

Save this before you download a model.
